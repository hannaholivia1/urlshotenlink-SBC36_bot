# Privacy Policy — SBC36 URL Shortener

**Last updated: September 2025**

## Introduction

SBC36 URL Shortener ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect

- **Telegram numeric user ID** — to track your shortening history
- **URLs you shorten** — stored temporarily to show your history

We do NOT collect:
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Location data
- Personal messages
- Any other personal information

## How We Use Your Data

- To shorten URLs you send
- To show you your personal shortening history
- Nothing else

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your data
- Share with third parties
- Track you outside the bot
- Send spam

## Third-Party Services

When you shorten a URL, the link is processed by **TinyURL** (tinyurl.com). TinyURL's own privacy policy applies to the shortened links.

## Data Storage

- User IDs and URL history are stored temporarily on Railway
- Data is removed when the service restarts
- No permanent storage

## Your Rights

- Stop using the bot at any time
- Block the bot to remove all data
- Contact us for data deletion

## Children's Privacy

This bot is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
